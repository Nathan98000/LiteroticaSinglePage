Browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave

Read Literotica stories on one page

Single-Page Reader joins every page of a multi-page story into one long page, so you can keep scrolling instead of clicking “Next page”. It works by itself: open a story and it does the rest.

ZIP file, __ZIP_KB__ KB, version __VERSION__

An independent, unofficial extension. It isn’t made by or affiliated with Literotica.

What it does

→
  • Automatic. There’s nothing to press. It starts as soon as you open a story.
  • Looks the same. The site’s layout, fonts and colours stay exactly as they are.
  • Short stories stay as they are. A story that’s already one page doesn’t change.
  • Private. It collects nothing and only talks to Literotica.

Install it

It takes about three minutes. Extensions that aren’t in the Chrome Web Store are added through Chrome’s Developer mode. That sounds technical, but it’s just a switch.

  1. Download it and unzip it

    Download the extension with the button below. Then open your Downloads folder and unzip the file: on a Mac, double-click it; on Windows, right-click it and choose Extract All. You’ll get a folder called literotica-single-page-reader.

  2. Move the folder somewhere safe

    Chrome runs the extension straight from this folder, so move it somewhere you won’t delete it by accident, such as your Documents folder. If the folder is deleted, the extension stops working.

  3. Open the extensions page and turn on Developer mode

    Copy this address, paste it into Chrome’s address bar, and press Enter. (Chrome doesn’t let web pages link to it, so it has to be typed or pasted.)

    chrome://extensions

    On that page, turn on the Developer mode switch in the top-right corner.

    Extensions Developer mode
    Load unpacked Pack extension Update

    Simplified drawing. Your screen will look a little different.

    Using Microsoft Edge or Brave? Paste edge://extensions or brave://extensions instead. In Edge, the Developer mode switch is in the menu on the left.

  4. Click “Load unpacked” and choose the folder

    Once Developer mode is on, a Load unpacked button appears. Click it, find the literotica-single-page-reader folder (it has a file called manifest.json directly inside), select the folder, and click Select (Select Folder on Windows).

    The extension then appears in the list, already switched on:

    __NAME____VERSION__ __DESCRIPTION__ DetailsRemove

    There’s no toolbar button and nothing to set up.

  5. Open a story

    Go to any Literotica story with more than one page. After a second or two, the rest of the story appears around the page you’re on. If you already had a story open, reload it first.

Using it

While it works, a small dark label appears in the bottom-right corner of the story page. This is what it can say.

Combining pages… 2 of 5
The other pages are loading. Keep reading: nothing on the page moves while you wait.
5 pages combined Paged view
Done. The whole story is on one page. Click Paged view to go back to the site’s normal pages. The label fades so it stays out of your way.
Page 1 of 5 Single page
You’re looking at the normal pages. Click Single page to join them again. It switches instantly.
Couldn’t find page 4 of 5. Showing the original pages. Retry ×
A page didn’t load, so you get the normal pages rather than a story with a gap in it. Click Retry to try again, or × to hide the message.

Good to know

  • Started on a later page? If you open page 3 of a story, you stay exactly where you are on page 3. Pages 1 and 2 are added above it, so scroll up to read them.
  • Each part of a series stays separate. On Literotica, every chapter or part is its own story. The extension joins the pages of the part you’re reading, not the whole series.
  • Paged view lasts until you leave. The next story you open is joined automatically again.

If something isn’t working

Chrome says “Manifest file is missing or unreadable”

You picked a folder one level too high. Open the folder you chose: if there’s another literotica-single-page-reader folder inside it, choose that inner one. The right folder has manifest.json directly inside it.

Nothing happens when I open a story
  • Check that the story really has more than one page. Single-page stories are left alone.
  • Reload the story page.
  • Open chrome://extensions and check that __NAME__ is switched on.
The red message keeps coming back

Literotica didn’t send one of the pages, usually because the connection dropped or the site is busy. Wait a moment and click Retry. You can always keep reading with the normal pages in the meantime.

Chrome shows a warning about Developer mode or this extension

Chrome sometimes reminds you about extensions that were added outside the Chrome Web Store. That’s expected for an extension installed this way. Leave it switched on to keep using it.

Does it work on a phone or tablet, or in Safari or Firefox?

No. It needs Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Brave on a Windows, Mac or Linux computer. Phone and tablet browsers don’t support extensions like this one.

What it can see

When you add it, Chrome says the extension can read and change your data on literotica.com sites. That’s what lets it join a story’s pages on the page you’re reading. Here’s what it does with that:

  • It only runs on Literotica, and only changes story pages.
  • It only downloads the other pages of the story you’re reading, from Literotica itself, just as your browser would when you click “Next page”.
  • It doesn’t collect, store or send anything anywhere else. It has no account and no settings.
  • It doesn’t get around logins, age checks or anything else the site asks of you.

Turning it off, removing it or updating it

All of these happen on the extensions page (chrome://extensions).

  • Turn it off for a while: flip the switch on its card off, and back on whenever you like.
  • Remove it: click Remove on its card. You can then delete the folder.
  • Install a newer version: remove the old one, delete its folder, then follow the install steps again with the new download.
